NETFLIX DATA ANALYSIS REPORT
========================================

BUSINESS INSIGHTS
----------------------------------------
- Movies account for 69.69% of the content library.
- TV Shows account for 30.31% of the content library.
- United States is the country with the highest number of titles.
- TV-MA is the most common content rating.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------
- Maintain strong movie content availability while continuing to develop the TV Show library.
- Prioritize internationally diverse content because International Movies is the most common genre.
- Consider content strategies around mature audiences because TV-MA is the most common rating.
- Use historical content-addition trends to plan future content acquisition and release strategies.
- Consider both short-form TV series and longer-running series when expanding the TV Show catalogue.
